The Experience

More than just a cup of coffee

Heights Coffee Bar was built around a simple idea: give Northeast Minneapolis and Columbia Heights a place that feels like it belongs to everyone. That means an acoustically-treated space with a sound system worth listening to, walls that rotate through local artists' work, and a room where strollers, laptops, and long conversations are all equally welcome.

Whether you're stopping in for a quick espresso or settling in for the afternoon, you'll find a thoughtfully curated, family-friendly environment that's proudly local — down to the last bean.

Acoustically-tuned space

A quality sound system and treated walls, so conversations and music both feel right.

Rotating local art

Our walls showcase Northeast Minneapolis artists, refreshed throughout the year.

Family-friendly & community-first

Welcoming neighbors from Columbia Heights, Northeast Minneapolis, and beyond.
